No precise quota is asserted without a retrieved official source. Monthly free credits and one-time trials are different allowances.

Local credit costs are conservative request reservations rather than an account quote. Actual provider charges, outside usage, plan restrictions, API rate limits and overage settings must be checked before live use. Keep paid auto-top-ups disabled where available. An access-confirmation flag never grants permission to spend paid credits.

Phone lookup is implemented only for Prospeo and GetProspect. No scraping, LinkedIn session automation, unlimited candidate sourcing, messages or CRM synchronization is included. Additional discovery and all validation/risk work are opt-in. Pending jobs do not poll automatically, and no scheduled enrichment is enabled.
